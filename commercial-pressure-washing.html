Commercial Pressure Washing Services Across Long Island, NY

The exterior of your business is your first impression. A dirty, stained, or algae-covered building communicates neglect — and on Long Island's competitive commercial landscape, that matters. Great Bay Pressure Wash provides professional commercial pressure washing services that keep your property clean, presentable, and compliant with property standards.

We work with businesses of all types across Long Island — from small retail storefronts in Sayville and Bohemia to large commercial facilities in Ronkonkoma and Central Islip. Our team is experienced with commercial-scale projects, and we schedule work before opening hours, after closing, or on weekends to minimize any disruption to your operations.

Recurring Commercial Maintenance Programs

Many Long Island businesses find that setting up a recurring maintenance agreement with Great Bay is the most cost-effective approach to keeping their property clean. We offer monthly, quarterly, and semi-annual programs that include:

  • Priority scheduling — your property is always on the calendar
  • Reduced pricing vs. one-time service rates
  • Consistent results that reflect well on your brand year-round
  • Single point of contact for all your exterior cleaning needs

Commercial clients note: A clean commercial exterior isn't just about aesthetics. Regular professional cleaning keeps you compliant and protects your property value.
