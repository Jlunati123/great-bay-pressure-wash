Privacy Policy

How Great Bay Pressure Wash collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective date: July 15, 2026
Last updated: July 15, 2026

Great Bay Pressure Wash (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a pressure washing company based in Oakdale, New York, serving homeowners and businesses across Long Island. This policy explains what information we collect through greatbaypressurewash.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you request a quote, call us, or fill out a form on this site, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your property address or service location
  • Details about the work you’re asking about

We ask for this so we can respond to you, prepare an accurate estimate, and schedule the job. We only ask for what we need to do that.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we collect some technical information automatically when you visit:

  • IP address and general location (typically city or region)
  • Browser type and device type
  • Pages you viewed, and how long you stayed
  • The site or ad that referred you here

Cookies and advertising technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies — including advertising and analytics pixels — to understand how the site is used and to show our ads to people who may be interested in our services. Depending on what is active at any given time, this site may use:

  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) — measures the results of our ads and lets us show ads to people who have visited this site. Data is shared with Meta Platforms, Inc.
  • Google Analytics — tells us how visitors find and use the site, in aggregate.
  • Google Ads — measures which ads lead to quote requests, and supports remarketing.

These tools may set cookies in your browser and share information about your visit with the companies that operate them. That means you may see our ads on Facebook, Instagram, Google, or YouTube after visiting this site. This is standard advertising practice and is commonly called remarketing.

We do not use these tools to collect sensitive information, and we do not knowingly send them your name, email, or phone number.

How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests and answer questions
  • To schedule, perform, and follow up on work
  • To send invoices and service communications
  • To improve our website and our services
  • To measure and improve our advertising

We do not sell your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or trade our customer list. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate — for example our website host, form and email providers, and scheduling or invoicing software — and only so they can perform that work for us.
  • Advertising and analytics platforms as described above.
  • Legal authorities, if we are required by law to do so.

Your choices

You have real options for limiting this tracking:

  • Browser cookie controls. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking them will not stop you from using this site.
  • Meta ad preferences. Adjust how Meta uses your activity at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Google ad settings. Adjust Google’s ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
  • Google Analytics opt-out. Install the browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Contact us directly. Ask us to delete the information you’ve given us, and we will.

How long we keep information

We keep quote requests and customer records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our tax and business record obligations. If you ask us to delete your information and we’re not required to keep it, we’ll delete it.

Security

This site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you give us. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we can’t promise absolute security.

Children’s privacy

This site is meant for adults seeking home and business services. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we’d encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business or the tools we use change. When we do, we’ll update the “last updated” date at the top of this page.
