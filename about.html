Our Story

Long Island Born. Long Island Proud.

Great Bay Pressure Wash was founded with a simple mission: provide Long Island homeowners and businesses with the highest quality exterior cleaning service they've ever experienced — at a fair price, with complete professionalism, and without the stress of dealing with an unreliable contractor.

We're based right here in Oakdale, NY. We live in these neighborhoods. Our kids go to school with yours. When we clean your home in Sayville or your business in Ronkonkoma, we're not just completing a job — we're taking care of our community.

That local pride drives everything we do, from showing up on time to following up after every job to make sure you're completely satisfied.

Our Approach

We Do It Right — Every Time

Anyone can buy a pressure washer. What separates a professional from an amateur is knowing when to use it — and when not to. Our technicians are trained to identify the right cleaning method for every surface:

  • Soft washing for roofs, siding, and delicate surfaces
  • High-pressure for concrete, asphalt, and hard surfaces
  • Appropriate chemical selection for each contaminant type
  • Proper protection of landscaping, windows, and fixtures
  • Thorough post-job cleanup and inspection

We invest in commercial-grade equipment and professional-grade cleaning solutions — not the consumer products you'd find at a hardware store. The difference in results is significant, and our customers notice it every time.
