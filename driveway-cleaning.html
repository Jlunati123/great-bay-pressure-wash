What We Remove from Long Island Driveways

  • Oil and grease stains — common in older Long Island homes with aging vehicles
  • Tire marks and rubber deposits
  • Embedded dirt and grime from years of vehicle traffic
  • Green algae and mold growth — especially in shaded areas
  • Salt residue from winter road treatments
  • Rust stains from fertilizers or irrigation water
  • Leaf tannin stains from overhanging trees
  • Efflorescence (white mineral deposits on concrete)

Concrete Driveway Cleaning

Concrete driveways are the most common surface in Long Island neighborhoods from Sayville to Ronkonkoma. Our concrete cleaning process uses a surface cleaner attachment — a rotating pressure washing head that delivers consistent, even cleaning across the entire surface. This eliminates the uneven "striping" you get from holding a single wand across concrete.

For heavy oil stains, we apply a commercial degreaser with dwell time before pressure washing. Most oil stains are dramatically improved or eliminated entirely. Deep, long-set stains may lighten significantly but may not be fully removed — we'll assess and be honest with you about expected results.

Asphalt Driveway Cleaning

Asphalt driveways require different pressure settings than concrete — too much pressure can dislodge the surface aggregate. Our technicians are trained to use the optimal pressure and technique for asphalt. We remove biological growth, dirt, and staining while preserving the integrity of your asphalt surface.

Driveway Sealing (Add-On Service)

After cleaning, we recommend considering professional paver or concrete sealing to protect your investment. A quality concrete sealer after pressure washing will:

  • Repel future oil and water staining
  • Slow algae and mold regrowth
  • Protect against freeze-thaw damage during Long Island winters
  • Give concrete a clean, finished appearance

How Long Does Driveway Cleaning Take?

A standard two-car concrete driveway in Oakdale or Sayville typically takes 1–2 hours to clean thoroughly. Longer driveways, circular drives, or heavily stained surfaces may take longer. We can provide a time estimate when you book.

Tip for Long Island homeowners: Schedule driveway cleaning in spring after winter salt residue has accumulated, or in fall before leaves stain the concrete. Pairing driveway cleaning with house washing gives you the best overall curb appeal improvement at the lowest combined cost.

Driveway Cleaning Pricing on Long Island

Driveway cleaning in the Long Island area typically ranges from $150–$350 depending on size, surface type, and staining. Single-car driveways start at around $150. We always provide a free written estimate.
