What Is Soft Wash House Washing?

Soft washing is the professional standard for cleaning residential exteriors. Instead of relying on brute force pressure that can dent vinyl, strip paint, or force water behind your siding, soft washing uses:

  • Low pressure — similar to a garden hose, safe on all surfaces
  • Professional-grade biodegradable cleaning solutions — specifically formulated to kill algae, mold, mildew, and bacteria
  • Proper dwell time — allowing the solution to break down contaminants completely before rinsing

The result is a deeper, longer-lasting clean compared to high-pressure washing alone. While pressure washing blasts off surface grime, soft washing actually kills the organisms causing the staining — meaning your home stays clean for 18–24 months versus just 3–6 months with pressure alone.

Signs Your Long Island Home Needs a Wash

Most homeowners in the Oakdale and Sayville area should have their homes washed at least once per year. Look for these signs it's overdue:

  • Black, green, or brown streaks running down your siding
  • Green algae or moss visible near the foundation or roof line
  • Chalky or dusty appearance on painted surfaces
  • Visible dirt accumulation in siding grooves or trim
  • Mold or mildew smell near exterior walls
  • Your neighbors just had their house cleaned and yours looks noticeably dirtier

The House Washing Process at Great Bay

When you book a house washing service with Great Bay Pressure Wash, here's what to expect:

  1. Pre-rinse: We wet down surrounding landscaping and cover sensitive plants to protect them during the cleaning process.
  2. Application: Our technician applies our professional cleaning solution to your siding from the bottom up, ensuring even coverage.
  3. Dwell time: We allow the solution time to penetrate and neutralize mold, algae, and biological growth.
  4. Low-pressure rinse: A thorough top-down rinse removes all contaminants and cleaning solution residue.
  5. Inspection: We walk the property to ensure every area is clean and no spots were missed.

Did you know? The black and green streaks you see on Long Island siding are almost always Gloeocapsa Magma — a type of algae that feeds on moisture and organic matter. Standard hosing won't remove it. Professional soft washing eliminates it completely.

Why Long Island Homes Get Dirty So Fast

If you've lived on Long Island for any length of time, you know our climate can be tough on homes. Our proximity to the Great South Bay and the Atlantic Ocean creates a humid coastal environment that's ideal for algae and mold growth. Add in our tree-heavy neighborhoods in Bohemia, Sayville, and Oakdale, and you have the perfect recipe for exterior buildup.

Regular annual house washing doesn't just improve appearance — it actually extends the life of your siding, paint, and caulking by preventing biological organisms from degrading the materials over time. Think of it as routine maintenance, just like you'd service your HVAC or clean your gutters.
