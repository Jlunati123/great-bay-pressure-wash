What Causes Those Black Streaks on Your Roof?

The dark staining you see on roofs across Long Island is caused by Gloeocapsa Magma — a type of blue-green algae that thrives in humid climates. It feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles and spreads via airborne spores. Without treatment, it will:

  • Continue spreading until it covers the entire roof
  • Reduce your shingles' ability to reflect UV rays (increasing energy costs)
  • Trap moisture, accelerating shingle deterioration
  • Lower your home's curb appeal and resale value
  • Eventually cause premature shingle failure

Moss and lichen cause even greater damage, physically lifting shingle granules as their root systems dig into the asphalt. Early intervention with professional soft washing can add years — even decades — to your roof's useful life.

Why Soft Washing, NOT Pressure Washing, for Roofs

This is critical: never pressure wash an asphalt shingle roof. High pressure blasts away the protective granule coating from shingles, voids your roof warranty, and can force water under shingles causing leaks and deck rot. We've seen many Long Island homes that were damaged by well-meaning homeowners or untrained contractors using pressure washers on roofs.

Our soft wash method applies a professional-grade, biodegradable cleaning solution at very low pressure (around 50–100 PSI — compare that to the 1,000–4,000 PSI of a standard pressure washer). The solution does the work, not the pressure. It kills the algae, mold, and lichen at the root level and rinses clean — leaving shingles completely intact and protected.

Our Roof Washing Process

  1. Property protection: We cover and pre-wet all landscaping, close all windows, and protect outdoor furniture and electrical fixtures.
  2. Application: We apply our biodegradable soft wash solution evenly across the entire roof surface from the ridge down.
  3. Dwell time: The solution is allowed to work for the appropriate time, fully neutralizing biological growth.
  4. Low-pressure rinse: We rinse the roof gently from top to bottom, removing all residue and organic matter.
  5. Post-rinse landscaping check: We re-rinse your plants and surrounding areas to neutralize any chemical runoff.

How Often Should You Have Your Roof Cleaned in New York?

For most Long Island homes, we recommend roof soft washing every 2–3 years as a maintenance schedule. Homes with heavy tree coverage, north-facing roof sections, or near the water in areas like Great River, Bayport, or Oakdale may benefit from annual cleaning due to increased moisture exposure.
